About the Doctor

About Dr. Ziad El Behansawy

Dr. Ziad El Behansawy is a professional dentist providing home dental care services for patients who need comfortable and accessible dental treatment at home. His goal is to make dental care easier, safer, and less stressful for elderly patients, children, patients with limited mobility, and anyone who needs urgent dental advice or follow-up at home.

Simple Process

How the Home Visit Works

1

Book Your Visit

The patient fills out a simple booking form or contacts the doctor.

2

Initial Assessment

Dr. Ziad reviews the complaint and checks if the case is suitable for home treatment.

3

Home Visit Preparation

Sterile instruments and necessary materials are prepared before the visit.

4

Dental Examination at Home

The doctor examines the patient and provides suitable care or advice.

5

Treatment Plan

The patient receives a clear plan and may be referred to a clinic if advanced treatment is needed.

Emergency Care

Dental Emergency Help

Severe toothache

Facial swelling

Broken tooth

Bleeding after extraction

Dental abscess

Gum pain

Important: If the patient has severe facial swelling, fever, difficulty swallowing, difficulty breathing, or uncontrolled bleeding, they should go to the nearest emergency hospital immediately.
